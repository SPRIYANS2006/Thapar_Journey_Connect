Privacy and safety

Thapar Journey Connect is a student travel coordination project. Do not assume it is an official TIET service. It does not book vehicles, verify drivers or companions, track arrivals, provide insurance, or dispatch emergency help. Institutional sign-in verifies control of an email account, not personal safety.

Information we store and share

The application stores your institutional account details, profile fields you provide, travel requests and memberships, messages, ratings, preferences and support reports. Relevant travel/profile information is shared with other users. Phone numbers for shared rides are shown to co-riders after confirmation or completion; joined train/bus group members can see each other's contact details. People can copy information already shared.

Provide another person's details only with their permission. Passwords are hashed; chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Session cookies restore sign-in and the theme preference may be stored in your browser.

Retention and requests

Automatic retention limits are not currently implemented. Records remain until the operator removes them. After signing in, use Safety & privacy to submit a correction, privacy/deletion request, report, or rating appeal. Requests require manual review and no response time is guaranteed. This inbox is not for emergencies.

Travel decisions

Slots represent co-traveller coordination, not vehicle availability. Fares and savings are estimates. Agree actual fares, stops, pickup and driver details independently. History inferred from departure time does not verify arrival. Regular campus ride cancellations/reschedules near departure may affect coordination scores; community rides currently have no automated deduction. Emergency or disputed deductions can be submitted for review.
